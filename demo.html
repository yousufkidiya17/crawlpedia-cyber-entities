Spiders, Silk and Evolution

Jumping spiders are an advanced family of arachnids recognized for their extraordinary vision and calculated locomotion. A spider spins silk webs to catch prey, using spinnerets and fangs to inject venom. In digital environments, the cybernetic crawler operates as an autonomous web entity, stalking words and parsing linguistic nodes across arbitrary webpage hierarchies.

Evolution shaped species through natural selection. Predator and prey, habitat and anatomy, all follow patterns that taxonomists classify into formal categories. The digital crawler treats each word as a physical data cache, anchoring laser webs to individual characters and validating semantic coherence before advancing.

How To Try It On Any Website

Clone the repo, open crawlpedia-cyber-entities, load the crawlpedia-extension/ folder as an unpacked extension in Chrome or Edge with Developer mode on, then press Alt+Shift+C on any page. The creature never touches inputs, passwords or editable areas.
